ACCC TELEMETRY — SHARED COMPARISON

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder.
2. Open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser.
3. Keep index.html and its accompanying files and folders together.

No installation, account, Python, server or internet connection is required.

REVIEW CONTROLS

• Play / pause: button next to the comparison review slider.
• Scrub: drag the review slider, move over the traces, or click the circuit map.
• Time / Distance: change the horizontal chart and video synchronization axis.
• Zoom: drag horizontally across the traces; double-click to reset.
  The zoom buttons and arrow keys on the chart also work.
• Source frame: click either video or its small thumbnail to enlarge it.
  In the enlarged view, move by one second or one thirtieth of a second,
  or type a source timestamp. Close the dialog to resume comparison.
• Swap: exchange your run and the reference with the arrow button.
• Single recording: clear one of the recording selectors; select it again
  to restore the paired comparison.
• Circuit: hover its colored sections for local time differences; use the
  expand button for a larger map.

This package reproduces the app's Analysis workspace using the saved lap
selections. Lap boundaries, recording labels, imports and automatic crossing
searches are fixed in this shared copy. Playback, chart navigation and review
remain interactive. The comparison calculations were exported from the app;
the package does not rerun video recognition.

The lap clocks and all video seeks retain the original source timestamps.
Speed, pedals and other channels are observations extracted from video;
distance and track position are estimates. Missing observations remain gaps.

If video does not appear, confirm that the whole ZIP was extracted and the
media folder is next to index.html. Opening HTML inside a ZIP preview will
not provide access to the videos. See Sources and credits at the bottom of
the page for the recordings and included track assets.
