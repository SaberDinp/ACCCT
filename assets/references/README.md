Checked 2026-11-13. The catalog contains **6,084 individual reference videos**, covering **51 of 54 cars** and **25 circuits**. There are **1,082 of 1,350 car-and-circuit combinations** with a reference; **975** have multiple video options.

The application lists every supported car and circuit, including combinations for which no verified reference has been found. The coverage table below records those gaps. This catalog contains links and metadata; it includes no video files.

## What has been checked

Each accepted link was returned by the creator’s public YouTube video, channel, or playlist metadata. Car and circuit identities come from the creator’s title, with specific creator tags or playlist names used only where they resolve an omitted model suffix. Generic Nissan/Bentley years and ambiguous Porsche generations remain outside the catalog unless the exact variant is stated.

Fri3d0lf and PS_Racing entries identify hotlaps in their titles. Coach Dave entries identify onboard laps. Ohne Speed entries also have explicit race/qualifying-lap chapters or description evidence. General setup guides, unrelated games, and titles without a circuit are excluded.

These are metadata checks. They do not establish that every full video has readable HUD telemetry, that each lap is valid, or that extraction will succeed. One Fri3d0lf opening storyboard was visually checked for speed, gear, and timer HUD; other entries retain `hud_verification: "not_reviewed"`. A creator’s lap time is a reported value, not an independent measurement. Video availability can change after the check date.

Five Coach Dave thumbnail samples were also inspected. Two show cockpit HUD elements; three are promotional images. These thumbnails do not establish input-bar readability or full-lap extraction. Further playback-image checks were stopped when YouTube returned a rate limit.

Track Titan's ACC series is excluded: representative storyboard images show custom overlays obscuring the standard HUD used by the current extractor. Exact car-and-circuit metadata alone was insufficient to include those videos.

Game versions and publication dates remain `null` when the inspected metadata does not state them. CDA3/CDA4/CDA M are setup editions and are stored as `setup_context`, never treated as game versions. Wet laps and LFM/BoP context remain labelled in titles and notes. Consider those conditions when choosing a comparison.

## Catalog schema

`catalog.json` contains `version: 1`, an ISO `updated_at` timestamp, and a `references` array. Each reference has:

- A unique stable `id`, `provider`, `video_id`, and canonical individual-video `url`.
- The exact creator `title` and a `creator` object with `name`, `channel_id`, and `url`.
- Exact `car_id` and `track_id` values from `assets/catalog/acc.json`.
- Optional `lap_time_seconds`, `lap_time_text`, `game_version`, `published_at`, and `duration_seconds`; unavailable values are `null`.
- `verified_at`, a `provenance` object identifying the primary source and matching method, `hud_verification`, and `notes`.
- Optional `setup_context` or `lap_chapters`. Chapters are video sections, not verified start/finish timestamps for a measured lap.

## Contributing or updating entries

1. Locate an individual public ACC hotlap/onboard on its original creator channel. Record the exact URL and title; a search-result or playlist URL is not an importable reference.
2. Match the exact car generation/class and circuit layout to the ACC roster. Resolve ambiguous titles from primary creator evidence or leave the candidate out. Preserve separate Evo, year, Cup, GT2, GT3, GT4, and Super Trofeo models.
3. Record creator identity, source URL, check date, and the field evidence. Retain the creator’s stated time/version and use `null` for missing information. Do not derive a lap time from a video’s duration or a chapter length.
4. Describe any visual HUD check precisely. Only use `hud_verification: "visible"` for inspected imagery; successful full-lap telemetry extraction requires separate verification.
5. Keep IDs and canonical video URLs unique. Validate all car/track IDs against the roster, update this coverage table, and run the reference-library tests before packaging.

Research snapshots and excluded/pending candidates for this update are retained locally under `artifacts/reference-research/`. They are not bundled video assets.
